# Thumbnail brief — EP001

## Recommended concept: WHY CHECK IT?

**Goal:** Make the viewer recognize the Costco exit ritual in under one second.

### Composition
- 16:9 YouTube thumbnail.
- Dark / near-black background matching Behind the Price.
- Left: oversized warehouse receipt, slightly tilted, with a bold black marker slash/check near the bottom.
- Right: simplified warehouse exit doorway / cart silhouette.
- Small electric-green accent line from the brand.
- Large text only: **WHY CHECK IT?**
- Keep the subject count low. No collage.

### Visual hierarchy
1. Receipt
2. "WHY CHECK IT?"
3. Exit/cart cue

### Important
- Do not use a fake screenshot of a Costco checkout system.
- If Costco signage/logo appears, use only an appropriately licensed editorial image or a minimal nominative reference.
- No shocked faces, arrows, circles, money rain, or generic AI-business imagery.
- Must remain readable on a phone at small size.

## Alternate A
**Text:** NOT FOR THEFT?
Receipt + security-camera icon crossed out + warehouse exit.

Risk: slightly more provocative and easier to misread, so only use if the final title/copy keeps the nuance.

## Alternate B
**Text:** YOU ALREADY PAID
Receipt + checkout line fading into an exit checkpoint.

More human and relatable, but weaker curiosity than **WHY CHECK IT?**

## Recommended title pairing
**Why Costco Checks Your Receipt Before You Leave**

Thumbnail text: **WHY CHECK IT?**

Do not repeat the full title on the thumbnail.
